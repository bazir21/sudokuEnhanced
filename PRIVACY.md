# Privacy Policy for BetterSudoku

**Effective date: October 31, 2026**

BetterSudoku is a browser extension for sudoku.com that adds keyboard
shortcuts, a dark mode, and a cleaner user interface. This policy explains
what data the extension handles. The short version: it collects nothing.

## What information is collected

BetterSudoku does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data.

The extension stores three categories of settings locally in your browser
using the WebExtension `browser.storage.local` API:

- whether dark mode is enabled,
- whether the cleaner UI is enabled,
- your custom keyboard shortcut bindings.

These settings never leave your device. They are not sent to the developer
or to any third party, and no server receives them.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, usage tracking, or telemetry.
- No accounts, sign-in, or personally identifiable information.
- No cookies and no advertising.
- No network requests of any kind.
- No remote code.

The extension's only permission is `storage`, used solely for the local
settings described above.

## How information is used

The local settings exist only to make the extension remember your
preferences between browser sessions. They are used for nothing else.

## Sharing and disclosure

Nothing is shared or disclosed, because nothing is collected or
transmitted.

## Your choices and data control

Because all data is stored locally, you are in full control of it:

- Uninstalling BetterSudoku removes its stored settings.
- You can also clear them at any time through your browser's extension
  storage settings.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
effective date. If a future version of the extension ever collects data,
this policy and the Chrome Web Store disclosures will be updated before
that version is released.
